# About

Abdulla Alshabanah is an Assistant Professor in the Computer Engineering Department at King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM). Before joining KFUPM in 2026, he was a researcher at Meta-USC research center. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from [the University of Southern California](https://www.usc.edu) in 2026, advised by [Prof. Murali Annavaram](http://scip-lab.usc.edu/). He also holds an M.S. in Computer Engineering, and an M.S. in Computer Science, both from the University of Southern California. His research interests include AI for Science, LLM-enhanced recommender systems, responsible and privacy-preserving AI, and efficient machine learning for large-scale model training.
